Calibrate NDT results against cores

Combine cores with paired NDT readings and further NDT readings across one test region to estimate its in-situ characteristic strength, following EN 13791:2019. You get two calibrations side by side: a local log-log regression on your cores, and the machine-learning model shifted to match your cores.
